Read the workflow as text

  1. Submit The Request

    The client submits the official job request form. The request arrives by email.

  2. Student Lead Logs The Job

    A student lead copies the emailed request into Planner, including the brief, client contact, resource links, first-draft date and final deadline.

  3. Assign & Plan

    Confirm the student owner. Meet with the client to agree on scope, approvals and dates. Create the Teams project folder and link it in Planner.

  4. Create & Track

    Use the Post-meeting checklist to create concepts and drafts. Save versioned files in Teams, keep the Planner task current and log work hours in DC Central.

  5. Review & Revise

    Collect client and DC leadership feedback. Communicate in Planner Task chat. Use @mentions to loop in teammates and emoji reactions to quickly share what you think. Revise until approved.

  6. Deliver & Close

    Deliver approved files and confirm completion by email. Keep editable sources and final exports in the project folder. Mark the Planner task complete.
